What Mind2Muscle Does

  • Start strength training sessions on Apple Watch or iPhone.
  • Record short voice moments after each set.
  • Audio is sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for fast, accurate transcription.
  • AI generates structured workout logs with exercises, sets, reps, and weights using GPT-4o.
  • Generate social content — Instagram captions, tweet threads, reel scripts.
  • Training insights and cross-workout analysis with Swift Charts.
  • Live heart rate and calorie tracking via HealthKit.
  • Edit your AI-generated workout log inline.
  • Offline recording — AI processing queues and retries when back online.
  • Sync workouts to Apple Health automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a workout?

Open Mind2Muscle on your Apple Watch and tap to start a session. You can also start a workout from the iPhone app.

Do I need an internet connection?

You can record voice moments offline — they'll be stored locally. However, transcription and AI processing require an internet connection since they use OpenAI's APIs. Everything queues up and processes automatically when you're back online.

How do I set up AI features?

Go to Settings in the iPhone app and enter your OpenAI API key. Once set, transcription and AI processing will run automatically when your workout ends.

Why isn't my transcription appearing?

  • Make sure your Apple Watch is paired with your iPhone.
  • Keep your iPhone nearby and open the app at least once.
  • Check that you have a valid OpenAI API key in Settings.
  • Confirm you have an internet connection for transcription.
  • Try recording a longer moment with clear speech.

What data does Mind2Muscle send to the cloud?

Audio recordings are sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription. Transcript text is then sent to OpenAI's GPT-4o for workout log generation, content creation, and insights. No other data leaves your device. There are no analytics or tracking.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. Mind2Muscle automatically syncs your workouts to Apple Health as strength training sessions, including duration and calorie data.

What HealthKit data does Mind2Muscle use?

Mind2Muscle reads live heart rate and active calories during your workout. When you end a session, it saves a strength training workout to Apple Health with duration, heart rate, and calorie data.

Can I edit my workout log after AI processing?

Yes. Tap Edit on any workout to modify exercises, sets, reps, or weights. Your changes are saved locally and won't be overwritten.
