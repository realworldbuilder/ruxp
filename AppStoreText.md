# RUXP — App Store text

**Subtitle:** You're not lifting alone.

**Promotional text:** Open RUXP before you lift. See how many people are training right now, join tonight's event, earn XP for every workout, level up through the season.

**Description:**

You might be alone at the gym on a Friday night. You are not lifting alone.

RUXP makes the gym feel like something is happening right now. Open it and see who is training, what's live tonight, and how close you are to your next level. Tap START WORKOUT, run a real Apple Watch strength session, talk through your sets, and finish to a screen that actually feels like something: XP earned, event bonus, level up.

- Live lifting count and live events, powered by Game Center
- Game Center leaderboards and achievements for XP, streaks, and season goals
- XP for finishing workouts, showing up weekly, hitting PRs, and joining events
- Seasons with a simple goal: 32 workouts, finish the season
- Apple Watch app for training: timer, voice notes, End, XP
- Voice notes become structured logs with your own OpenAI key
- Workouts save to Apple Health

No feed. No followers. Just the place you open when you're about to lift.

**Keywords:** lifting, weightlifting, strength, workout, gym, XP, season, apple watch, voice

**Note:** Live counts are real Game Center players. Expect small numbers until the community grows.
