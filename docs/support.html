Common questions

How do I get access?

Season 0 is invite-only while we ramp up. Use the Request access button on the home page and tell us a little about how you train.

How do I turn off Game Center?

Profile › Settings › Sync to Game Center. When it's off, nothing is submitted to Game Center.

How do I export or delete my data?

Profile › Settings › Export All Workouts produces a JSON file you can save anywhere. Delete All Data removes everything from the device. Deleting the app does the same. See the privacy policy for what leaves the device and when.

The Apple Watch app won't start a workout.

The watch needs Health permission for workouts. Accept the prompt the first time you tap Start, or allow it under the watch's Settings › Health › Apps. Open the iPhone app once first so the two can pair.

Report a bug

Include your iPhone and watchOS versions, what you tapped, and what you expected. Screenshots help. TestFlight users can shake the phone to send feedback with a screenshot attached.
