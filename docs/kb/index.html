The loop

Every screen serves one loop. If a feature does not improve it, it is not built.

flowchart LR
  A[OPEN] --> B[SEE WHAT'S HAPPENING]
  B --> C[START WORKOUT]
  C --> D[TALK THROUGH SETS]
  D --> E[END · EARN XP]
  E --> F[SEE WHO TRAINED WITH YOU]
  F --> G[COME BACK]
  G --> A

Underneath is the Momentary engine: a real HealthKit strength session, voice notes transcribed and parsed into exercises, sets, reps, weight, and personal records. On top is the product layer that makes the gym feel like a place: real presence counts, two weekly rituals, seasons, a free pass, live sessions, and a world that keeps moving while you are gone.

The standard

01 something is happening right now.
02 something happened while you were gone.
03 something new might happen tonight.

That is the whole design philosophy in three lines. The long version, the fifteen-question Aliveness Test, and the list of things RUXP refuses to do are on the philosophy page.

What's live in build 5

Right now

Real "lifting now" and "trained today" counts from Game Center, a featured ritual card, a LIVE dot, and a one-line clock under the Home wordmark.

While you were gone

After six hours away, Home lists what changed: rituals that ran, a friend who leveled up, your rank move, the new week, the season closing in, new players. Nothing invented.

Rituals

FRIDAY NIGHT (Fri 5 PM to midnight) and SUNDAY RESET (all Sunday), +500 XP each, with a lobby, a live join count, friends, and a reactions-only training room.

Seasons

S00 EARLY ADOPTERS closes Nov 30. The first launch after that shows a recap and freezes a record. S00 cosmetics stay yours forever. S01 PRESS START starts Dec 1.

Rules, not features

Live Ops: dated rules that change how XP is earned for a window. Three are scheduled for S00. They can be changed by editing one JSON file on this site.

Identity

Game Center name, Season Pass title, name color and badge, "SINCE SEP 2026", and a season history on the player card.

Crew

Your Game Center friends who lift. Who is lifting right now, who is in this week, and CREW WEEK: everyone trains once by Sunday midnight, +250 to each. Someone is expecting you.

  • Voice-logged workouts, PR detection, HealthKit sessions (Momentary engine)

  • Real Game Center presence, four ranking boards, twelve achievements
