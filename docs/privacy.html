RUXP for iPhone and Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026
  • No account. No sign-up. No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Voice notes you record during a workout are sent to OpenAI to be transcribed and turned into a workout log.
  • Apple Health (HealthKit) data never leaves your device and is never sent to OpenAI or anyone else.
  • Game Center is optional. When it is on, your XP totals, streak, achievements, and an "I'm training" ping are shared with Apple.
  • Everything else stays on your iPhone. Delete the app and it's gone.

Who we are

RUXP is an independent app made by one developer. There is no company behind it collecting data, no advertising, and nothing is sold, rented, or shared beyond what this page describes. Questions go to admin@ruxp.app.

What the app stores on your device

Your workouts (date, duration, exercises, sets, reps, weight), the voice notes you record and their transcripts, personal records, XP, level, streaks, season progress, equipped cosmetics, and your preferences. All of it lives in the app's private storage on your iPhone. The Apple Watch app keeps only the workout in progress until it hands it to the phone.

Voice notes and OpenAI

When you talk during a workout, RUXP records short audio clips. Each clip is sent to OpenAI's audio transcription API and returned as text. When the workout ends, the transcript text is sent to OpenAI's chat completion API, which returns a structured log (exercises, sets, reps, weight) that RUXP uses to detect personal records and award XP. Transcript text may also be sent to generate short training insights and to power the in-app coach chat.

What is sent: the audio of your voice notes and the text derived from them, plus workout details drawn from those notes (exercise names, sets, reps, weight, dates, durations). What is not sent: anything from Apple Health, your name, your Game Center identity, your location, or any device identifier.

OpenAI processes this data under its API data usage policies. As of this policy's effective date, OpenAI does not use API data to train its models and retains it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. An internet connection is required; without one, notes are queued on your device and processed later.

During Season 0 (September 1 to November 30, 2026) the app includes a developer-provided OpenAI key so these features work without any setup. You can replace it at any time with your own key in Profile › Settings; your key is stored in the iOS Keychain and sent only to api.openai.com. Removing your key returns the app to the developer-provided one.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

RUXP asks permission to write strength-training workouts to Apple Health and to read workouts so it can show your history. Health data is used only to run and record workouts on your device. It is never sent to OpenAI, to us, or to any third party, and it is never used for advertising or shared with data brokers. HealthKit data is governed by Apple's privacy policies and your Health app permissions, which you can change at any time in Settings › Health.

Game Center

RUXP uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards, achievements, and the live "lifting now" counts. It is optional and can be turned off in Profile › Settings. When it is on, the app submits your lifetime XP, season XP, longest week streak, achievement progress, and a timestamp-only "I'm training" score to short rolling leaderboards. Live counts are read back from those leaderboards as player totals; no one sees what you lifted. Your Game Center profile is managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; RUXP does not receive your Apple ID.

Microphone

Used to record voice notes during a workout on iPhone and on Apple Watch. Nothing is recorded outside an active workout. Watch recordings are transferred to your paired iPhone for transcription.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. There are no third-party SDKs in the app at all.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no request for tracking permission.
  • No accounts, passwords, email addresses, or contact lists.
  • No location.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app. Profile › Settings › Delete All Data removes every workout, transcript, and progress record. Deleting the app does the same. Audio clips are kept alongside their workout so you can replay them; they are deleted with the workout. Data already processed by OpenAI is subject to OpenAI's retention described above. Scores submitted to Game Center are managed through your Game Center account.

Children

RUXP is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
