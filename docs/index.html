How it works

The whole loop.

OPEN→SEE WHO'S TRAINING→START WORKOUT→EARN XP→FINISH→COME BACK

Live counts are real

"Lifting now" and "trained today" come from Game Center. An active workout pings a rolling leaderboard; the number of players in that window is the count. No server, no inflation.

XP

XP for healthy habits

+500 for a finished workout (10 minutes or more, two a day max). +500 for your fourth workout of the week. +250 per PR. Event bonuses on Friday Night and Sunday Reset.

⌚

Apple Watch is for training

Start a real strength session from your wrist. Timer, voice notes, End, then XP earned. The phone does the thinking; the watch just runs the workout.
