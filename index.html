Mind2Muscle

A workout notebook for your wrist

Talk to your Watch between sets. AI handles the rest — structured logs, training insights, even social posts.

On TestFlight Now
Status: Currently testing on TestFlight — actually using it at the gym. This is a personal project, you're seeing it early.

What It Does

🎙️

Voice Moments

Record on your Watch or iPhone between sets. Say what you did and move on — AI takes care of the rest.

🤖

AI Workout Logs

Your voice gets transcribed and processed by AI into a structured workout log — exercises, sets, reps, weights. Edit anything it gets wrong.

❤️

Live Health Data

Heart rate and calories tracked live during your session. Saves to Apple Health as a strength training workout.

📊

Insights

See trends across workouts — volume, frequency, muscle groups. Weekly stats and a story viewer to swipe through.

📱

Social Content

AI writes Instagram captions, tweet threads, and reel scripts from your session. Post-worthy without the effort.

⚙️

Works Offline

No signal at the gym? Recording still works. AI processing queues up and runs when you're back online.

How It Works

1

Start a workout on your Watch. Between sets, tap record and say what you did — "bench press, 4 sets of 8 at 185."

2

Audio transfers to your iPhone and gets sent to OpenAI's Whisper for fast, accurate transcription.

3

End your workout and AI puts it all together — a structured log, training insights, and ready-to-post social content.
